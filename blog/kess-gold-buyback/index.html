How Does KESS Gold Buyback Work?

The Special BuyBack contract is KESS/SSGC's formal commitment to repurchase your gold at the current market price. Here is exactly how it works — step by step.

Key takeaway: KESS/SSGC's buyback is not a loyalty programme or a marketing promise — it is a written contract. When you buy gold through the platform, you receive a Special BuyBack contract that legally commits KESS/SSGC to repurchase your gold at the prevailing market price.

What Is the Special BuyBack Contract?

When you purchase gold through KESS (Kedai Emas Seri Semantan) / SSGC, the transaction comes with a Special BuyBack contract — a formal written agreement issued alongside your purchase receipt.

The contract specifies:

  • The gold's weight (in grams)
  • The gold's purity (916 or 999)
  • The commitment to repurchase at prevailing market price
  • KESS/SSGC's registered business details

This written commitment distinguishes KESS/SSGC from informal resellers who offer buyback as a verbal promise but are not legally bound to follow through.

What the Special BuyBack Contract Covers

Gold weight and purity as verified at purchase (XRF-tested)
KESS/SSGC's commitment to repurchase at the prevailing market gold price
The gold's item type (jewellery or bar) and description
Branch details and date of original transaction
Member's details (name, IC/passport)

How the Buyback Process Works — Step by Step

  1. Gather your documents
    Bring your original Special BuyBack contract and your gold purchase receipt. These documents prove ownership and confirm the gold's original specifications. Keep them in a safe place from day one.
  2. Visit any KESS/SSGC branch
    Walk into any KESS/SSGC branch during operating hours. You are not restricted to the branch where you originally purchased the gold. Any branch can process your buyback.
  3. Present your gold and documents
    Hand over your gold piece(s) together with the Special BuyBack contract and receipt. The branch staff will check the documents against their records to confirm the transaction.
  4. XRF verification
    The gold is tested using an XRF (X-ray fluorescence) machine that reads the gold's purity to 3 decimal places. This confirms the piece matches the original specifications in your contract. The test takes under a minute and is performed in front of you.
  5. Weight confirmation
    The gold is weighed on a calibrated scale. The weight must match the original contract. Any significant discrepancy (scratches or minor wear are normal; material removal is not) would be discussed at this point.
  6. Receive the buyback price
    The buyback amount is calculated using the current market gold price at that moment, multiplied by the confirmed weight, adjusted for purity. Payment is made by the branch according to their standard payment methods.

What Price Do You Get?

The buyback price is based on the current market gold rate at the time of your transaction — not a fixed price set at purchase, and not a discounted "buyback rate" that is significantly lower than market.

Practically speaking:

  • If gold prices have risen since your purchase, you receive more ringgit than you paid for the gold's market value
  • If gold prices have fallen, the buyback amount reflects the lower current price
  • The spread (buy price vs. sell price at the same moment) is part of how KESS/SSGC operates as a business — this is disclosed upfront and is comparable to any licensed gold dealer's spread
Preloved advantage at buyback time: Members who bought preloved gold at KESS/SSGC (without paying a making charge) start at a lower cost basis. When you sell back at market price, your effective return is higher than if you had bought new jewellery of the same weight — because you avoided the RM15–50/gram making charge at the start.

Can You Sell Your KESS Gold Elsewhere?

Yes. The gold you hold is physically yours. It is real 916 or 999 gold that any licensed gold dealer in Malaysia, pawnshop (pajak gadai), or gold trader can buy. Your KESS/SSGC contract does not restrict you from selling elsewhere.

However, when selling to another dealer:

  • They will assess the gold on its own merits (weight, purity, their own spread)
  • Your KESS Special BuyBack contract is not relevant to them — it is an agreement with KESS/SSGC only
  • Prices will vary between dealers depending on their spread and business model

For most members, selling back to KESS/SSGC is the most straightforward path because the contract is designed for exactly this transaction and both parties have documented records.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does KESS gold buyback work?
Bring your gold, your Special BuyBack contract, and your purchase receipt to any KESS/SSGC branch. The branch verifies the gold using XRF testing, confirms the weight, and pays you the current market gold price for the gold's weight and purity. The whole process typically takes 15–30 minutes.
What is the KESS Special BuyBack contract?
A written legal commitment from KESS/SSGC to repurchase your gold at the prevailing market price. It is issued with every gold purchase and specifies the gold's weight, purity, and the buyback commitment. It is not just a verbal promise — it is a formal document.
What price do I get when I sell gold back to KESS?
The prevailing market gold price at the time of the transaction, multiplied by the gold's weight and adjusted for purity. If gold prices have risen since your purchase, you receive more. The spread between buy and sell prices is part of how KESS/SSGC operates as a business.
Can I sell my KESS gold at any time?
Yes — visit any branch during operating hours. There is no lock-in period preventing you from selling. The buyback price simply reflects the current gold market rate at the time you sell.
Do I need my original receipt and contract for the buyback?
Yes — bring both documents. They verify your ownership and the gold's original specifications. The XRF test at buyback also re-confirms the gold's purity independently.
Can I sell KESS gold at another gold shop?
Yes. Your gold is physically yours and any licensed dealer can buy it. The Special BuyBack contract only applies to KESS/SSGC branches — other dealers will assess the gold independently at their own rates.
