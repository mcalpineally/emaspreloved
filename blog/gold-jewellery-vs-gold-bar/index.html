The Core Difference: Making Charges

When you buy new gold jewellery in Malaysia, you pay two things: the gold's market value (based on weight and purity) and a making charge — the craftsman's fee for shaping the gold. Making charges typically run RM15–50 per gram depending on the design's complexity.

The critical problem: when you sell or trade in that jewellery, the shop pays you based on the gold's weight and market price only. The making charge is not refunded. You absorb it entirely.

Gold bars have no equivalent to making charges. The only additional cost is a small minting fee (usually RM5–15 per bar) that is negligible at scale.

When Gold Bars Win

Gold bars are the superior choice when:

  • You are building a pure investment portfolio and have no interest in wearing gold
  • You want to accumulate large quantities efficiently (bars eliminate the per-piece making charge drag)
  • You prefer the highest purity (999.9) for maximum gold content per ringgit
  • You are storing in a secure vault or safe deposit box — bars stack efficiently
  • You plan to sell in bulk — bars are easier to price and transact at scale

When Preloved Jewellery Wins

Preloved 916 gold jewellery is the superior choice when:

  • You want wearable gold that also holds investment value
  • You can access a trusted licensed platform (KESS/SSGC) with XRF verification and formal receipts
  • You want to give gold as a gift — jewellery has immediate lifestyle appeal gold bars do not
  • You prefer smaller denomination purchases — jewellery pieces come in varied weights
  • You want the investment efficiency of bars with the wearability of jewellery

What About New Jewellery as an Investment?

Buying new gold jewellery specifically as an investment is the least efficient path. The making charge means you need gold prices to rise by roughly 6–10% (the making charge percentage) just to break even on resale. This is not impossible — gold often rises that much in a year — but it is an avoidable handicap.

If someone gives you new gold jewellery as a gift or inheritance, hold it — the gold value is real and it tracks the market. But going to a shop and buying new jewellery as an "investment" is not the most rational approach when preloved options exist on licensed platforms.

Storage — A Practical Consideration

Gold bars require more deliberate storage planning than jewellery:

  • Bank safe deposit box: RM50–200/year — the standard for serious gold bar savers
  • Home safe: One-time cost, convenient but carries home theft risk
  • Platform storage: KESS/SSGC stores your gold at their branches — removes the personal storage concern entirely

Jewellery is naturally worn or stored in a jewellery box — most people already have a solution for it without additional cost.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is gold jewellery or gold bar a better investment in Malaysia?
For pure investment efficiency, gold bars win — minimal overhead, maximum gold content. New jewellery is least efficient due to making charges. Preloved jewellery is as efficient as bars and also wearable — often the best all-round choice.
What is a gold making charge and why does it matter?
The making charge is the craftsman's fee for shaping gold into jewellery — RM15–50/gram. It is not recovered on resale. On a 10-gram necklace, that is RM150–500 you absorb on day one. Gold bars have no equivalent cost.
What is the difference between 916 gold jewellery and 999 gold bars?
916 is 91.6% pure (22-karat) — used for jewellery, harder and more durable. 999 is 99.9% pure (24-karat) — used for bars and investment products, softer and more intensely yellow. Both are universally accepted for resale in Malaysia.
How do I store gold bars safely in Malaysia?
Common options: bank safe deposit box (RM50–200/year), home safe, or platform storage at KESS/SSGC branches. Platform storage is the most hands-off approach as the platform manages custody on your behalf.
Should I buy preloved gold jewellery instead of a gold bar?
If you want dual-purpose gold (wearable + investment), preloved 916 jewellery from a licensed platform is an excellent choice — no making charge, same resale value as new, with the added benefit of wearing it. For pure investment, bars are marginally more efficient per ringgit.
