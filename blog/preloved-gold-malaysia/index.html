What Is Preloved Gold?

Preloved gold — also called emas terpakai or second-hand gold in Malaysia — is gold jewellery that has been previously owned and is now resold at a price based on the gold's market value. This includes rings, necklaces, bangles, pendants, and earrings, most commonly in 916 purity (22 karat), which is Malaysia's most popular gold standard.

The key distinction from buying new jewellery: no fabrication fee (upah). When you buy new gold from a jewellery shop, the price includes the cost of crafting the piece — typically RM 15–50 per gram depending on design complexity. The original buyer paid this fee. When the piece enters the secondhand market, only the gold's market value is priced in.

The core benefit: Every gram of preloved 916 gold contains exactly the same amount of pure gold as an equivalent new piece. The purity is verified by XRF testing. The only difference is the price — which is lower because fabrication costs are absent.

How XRF Testing Works — and Why It Matters

XRF — X-ray Fluorescence Gold Testing
1
The piece is placed against the XRF gun's sensor — no scratching, no acid, no damage to the gold.
2
The machine emits X-rays that excite atoms in the metal. Each element emits a characteristic fluorescence signal.
3
The XRF reads back the exact composition: for example, "Gold 91.6%, Silver 5.1%, Copper 3.3%." This is 916 gold.
4
The result cannot be faked by gold plating — XRF reads the full depth of the metal, not just the surface layer.
5
The dealer prints the XRF certificate with the piece's weight, composition, test date, and market price. This becomes your proof of purchase.

An XRF machine costs RM 30,000–80,000 — only established, serious dealers invest in one. If a preloved gold seller cannot show you an XRF certificate or test the piece in front of you, do not transact.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is preloved gold in Malaysia?
Preloved gold (emas terpakai) is gold jewellery previously owned by someone else, now resold at a price based on the gold's current market value — without the fabrication fee charged when new jewellery is made. It is typically 916 (22K) purity and XRF-verified before sale.
Is it safe to buy preloved gold in Malaysia?
Yes, when purchased from an SSM-registered, licensed dealer that uses XRF testing. The key protection is the XRF certificate — it confirms the exact gold content. Always insist on an official receipt and an XRF test result before paying.
How much cheaper is preloved gold compared to new gold in Malaysia?
Typically RM 15–50 per gram cheaper — entirely because the fabrication fee (upah) paid by the original buyer is not carried forward. The gold itself is priced at or near the current market rate in both cases. For a 10g piece, the saving is around RM 150–500.
What is XRF testing for gold in Malaysia?
XRF (X-ray fluorescence) testing uses X-rays to identify the exact metal composition of a gold piece without scratching it. It measures how much gold, silver, and copper are in the alloy. For 916 gold, the result should show approximately 91.6% gold. It cannot be deceived by surface plating — it reads the full depth of the metal.
What are the red flags when buying preloved gold in Malaysia?
Price 20%+ below market rate (may be lower purity or fake), no XRF test, no official receipt, seller not SSM-registered, cash-only payment, delivery-only without in-person verification, and any pressure to decide quickly. Legitimate sellers have nothing to hide — they will test in front of you.
