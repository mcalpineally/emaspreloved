If you've come across the name SSGC, KESS, or Emas Sri Semantan and want a clear English explanation — this guide covers everything: what the community is, how the gold savings system works, and what the rebrand from SSPC to SSGC means.

What is SSGC / KESS?

SSGC stands for Sri Semantan Global Community. It is the affiliate and member community of KESS — Komuniti Emas Sri Semantan — a physical gold savings platform operated by SS Global Strategic Solutions Sdn Bhd.

In plain terms: SSGC is the name of the people's community, and KESS is the name of the gold savings system they participate in. The two names are often used interchangeably in everyday conversation.

Quick summary: Through KESS, members purchase 999.9 pure gold at the market price, store it at an authorised branch, and can eventually sell it back through a guaranteed premium buyback scheme. Members also earn points (SCP) through savings and referrals, which can be withdrawn as cash.

Why SSPC became SSGC (the 2026 rebrand)

Until recently, the community was known as SSPC — Sri Semantan Platinum Community. In 2026, the name was changed to SSGC — Sri Semantan Global Community.

The rebrand reflects the platform's expansion beyond Malaysia — the word "Platinum" was replaced with "Global" to signal an international outlook. The parent company (SS Global Strategic Solutions) and the KESS gold savings system itself did not change; only the community's name did.

What is Special BuyBack (SBB)?

The Special BuyBack (SBB) is KESS's signature feature — and what sets it apart from a simple gold savings account.

When a member activates an SBB contract, they agree to a fixed-term arrangement (typically 6 months). At maturity, KESS buys back their gold at a guaranteed premium above the spot price. The premium is stated in USD per gram at the time of activation.

SBB Umrah Package (2026): A specialised SBB option for members who want to perform Umrah. Minimum 50g of gold, 6-month contract, with a premium of USD 3.67 per gram. Eligible members may apply the buyback proceeds toward an SSGC-facilitated Umrah package.

SBB is not available to all members at all times — activation depends on your holding level and account standing. Speak to your upline or visit your nearest SSGC branch for current eligibility criteria.

What is SCP (Semantan Commodity Point)?

SCP — Semantan Commodity Point — is the internal reward currency of the KESS ecosystem. The key facts:

  • 1 SCP = 1 USD in value
  • Earned through gold savings milestones and community referrals
  • Can be withdrawn or used through 5 channels (fully synchronised from December 2026)

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between SSPC and SSGC?

SSPC (Sri Semantan Platinum Community) was the old name. In 2026 it was rebranded to SSGC (Sri Semantan Global Community). The gold savings system (KESS), the parent company (SS Global Strategic Solutions), and your existing account all remain unchanged — only the community's name changed.

Is KESS / SSGC a scam or legitimate?

SS Global Strategic Solutions Sdn Bhd is a registered Malaysian company. Members purchase and physically hold 999.9 gold through the platform. As with any financial or commodity programme, we recommend verifying the company's current SSM registration status before committing, understanding the full terms of any SBB contract, and speaking to existing members about their experience. This article does not constitute financial advice.

What does "Assistant Manager" mean in SSGC?

Assistant Manager is the entry-level affiliate rank when you first register as an SSGC member. As your gold savings grow and you build a referral network, you can advance to higher ranks (Manager, Senior Manager, and so on), which unlock higher SCP earning rates and additional benefits.

Can I withdraw my gold at any time?

Standard gold savings (outside an active SBB contract) can generally be liquidated through the buyback process at the branch. Members under an active SBB contract are subject to the contract terms — early termination conditions vary. Always check your current contract terms with your branch or upline before making a withdrawal decision.

How is SSGC different from a bank gold savings account?

Bank gold savings accounts (such as Maybank Gold Investment Account or BSN MyGold) hold paper/digital gold — you do not physically own the gold. With KESS, you purchase and hold physical 999.9 gold stored at a branch. The SBB scheme also offers a premium above spot price at maturity, which bank gold accounts typically do not offer. The trade-off is that KESS is a newer, smaller platform compared to established banks.

When will the 5 SCP withdrawal channels be fully available?

All 5 SCP withdrawal channels — cash, IE Bank, USDT exchanger, Visakad SS Pay, and 50% SCV credit — are scheduled to be fully synchronised and operational from December 2026. Check your SSGC member portal or ask your upline for the latest activation status.

What is the SBB Umrah Package?

The SBB Umrah Package is a specialised Special BuyBack option where eligible members commit a minimum of 50 grams of gold to a 6-month SBB contract with a premium of USD 3.67 per gram. Upon maturity, proceeds can be applied toward an Umrah travel package facilitated through the SSGC ecosystem. Contact your upline or the nearest SSGC branch for eligibility and availability.
