If you've seen the letters SCP mentioned on KESS materials, in your SSGC member portal, or by your upline — and wondered what they actually mean — this guide explains everything in plain English.

SCP is at the heart of how KESS rewards its members beyond the gold itself. Understanding it properly helps you plan when and how to withdraw your earnings.

What is SCP?

SCP stands for Semantan Commodity Point — the internal reward currency of the KESS (Komuniti Emas Sri Semantan) gold savings ecosystem, operated by SS Global Strategic Solutions Sdn Bhd under the SSGC community.

The most important thing to know about SCP:

1 SCP = 1 USD

SCP is pegged to the US dollar — not the Malaysian Ringgit. This means its RM value fluctuates with the USD/MYR exchange rate, but its purchasing power within the KESS platform is always 1 USD per point.

Think of SCP as a loyalty points system — except instead of airline miles you can only spend on flights, SCP can be converted to actual cash (RM or USDT), loaded onto a prepaid card, or used as credit for platform services.

SCP vs SCV — what's the difference?

These two abbreviations confuse many new KESS members. Here's the clear distinction:

SCP
Semantan Commodity Point
  • The point unit — 1 SCP = 1 USD
  • Lives in your member account balance
  • Earned through savings and referrals
  • Can be converted via 5 withdrawal channels
  • Think of it as your balance
SCV
Semantan Commodity Voucher
  • The voucher form of SCP value
  • Used for specific platform transactions
  • Not cashable on its own
  • Used as 50% credit for SBB, Umrah, travel, maintenance
  • Think of it as a store credit voucher

In practice: your SCP balance in the member portal is what you've earned. When you use the 50% SCV channel to pay for an SBB contract or Umrah package, that is KESS converting a portion of your SCP into SCV vouchers applied directly to that transaction — you don't hold SCV separately; it is used in the moment of the transaction.

How do you earn SCP?

SCP accumulates in your KESS account through two main routes:

SourceHow it worksWho qualifies
Gold savings SCP is credited based on your gold savings milestones — the more gold you hold through KESS, the more SCP you earn over time. All members
Community referrals When you introduce new members to SSGC and they register and save through your referral link, you earn SCP based on their activity within the network structure. All members
Rank advancement bonuses As you advance beyond Assistant Manager rank, your SCP earning multipliers increase. Higher ranks earn more SCP per gram saved and per referral. From Manager rank up
Special promotions KESS periodically runs promotions that award bonus SCP for specific actions — such as activating an SBB contract or reaching a savings milestone within a timeframe. Promotional periods only

Important: KESS does not publicly publish a fixed SCP earning rate table (e.g. "X SCP per gram"). The exact rates depend on your rank, your network structure, and prevailing promotions. Your upline is the best source for current earning rates specific to your account level.

Which channel should you use?

It depends on your situation:

  • Need cash in hand fast: Cash withdrawal at branch (RM1k/transaction)
  • Prefer bank transfer: IE Bank channel (RM1k/transaction, digital)
  • Hold or use crypto: USDT exchanger (125 SCP/transaction)
  • Spend regularly on daily purchases: Visakad SS Pay (RM1k/day loading)
  • Planning an SBB contract or Umrah: 50% SCV — reduces your cash outlay by half

Members with large SCP balances often combine channels — for example using 50% SCV to reduce SBB costs while withdrawing the remainder via IE Bank transfer.

Transaction limits are per transaction, not per day (except Visakad SS Pay, which is per day). If you have more than RM1,000 worth of SCP to withdraw via cash or IE Bank, you can make multiple transactions — but check with your upline or branch on any daily or monthly caps that may apply to your account level.

Frequently asked questions

What is SCP in KESS in simple terms?

SCP (Semantan Commodity Point) is KESS's internal reward currency. Every 1 SCP you earn is worth 1 USD. You earn SCP by saving gold through KESS and by referring new members. You can convert SCP to cash, bank transfer, USDT, a prepaid card, or use it as credit for platform purchases.

What is the difference between SCP and SCV?

SCP is your point balance — 1 SCP = 1 USD — sitting in your member account, earnable and withdrawable. SCV (Semantan Commodity Voucher) is a voucher form of SCP value used specifically for KESS platform transactions. You can't withdraw SCV as cash; it's applied as a discount (up to 50%) when paying for SBB contracts, Umrah packages, travel, or maintenance through KESS.

How do I withdraw my SCP as cash?

Visit your nearest SSGC branch with your IC and member account details. Request a cash withdrawal from your SCP balance. The maximum per transaction is RM1,000. The RM amount is calculated at the prevailing USD/MYR exchange rate on the day of withdrawal. This channel is fully available from December 2026.

Can I withdraw SCP to my Maybank / CIMB account?

The IE Bank transfer channel allows SCP to be sent to a Malaysian bank account (max RM1,000 per transaction). Confirm with your upline or branch which Malaysian banks are supported through IE Bank for SCP transfers, as the list may expand over time.

What is Visakad SS Pay?

Visakad SS Pay is a Visa prepaid card issued within the SSGC ecosystem. You can load it with SCP (max RM1,000 per day) and use it anywhere Visa is accepted — grocery stores, online shopping, petrol stations, etc. It's the most flexible channel for spending SCP without first converting to cash.

Does SCP expire?

SCP expiry terms are set by KESS and may apply depending on your account status. Contact your upline or visit a SSGC branch to check whether your SCP balance has an expiry date, especially if your account has been inactive for an extended period.

Can I earn SCP if I don't refer anyone?

Yes. SCP can be earned through your own gold savings milestones alone, without referring anyone. Referral-based SCP earnings are additional — they are not mandatory to participate in the savings system. That said, members who build a referral network earn SCP significantly faster than those who save solo.
