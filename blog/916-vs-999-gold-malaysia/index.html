916 vs 999 Gold — Which Should You Buy in Malaysia?

Both are real gold. The right choice depends on what you want the gold to do: be worn every day, store value, or be given as a gift. Here is how to decide.

What You Pay Per Gram

Because 999 holds about 9% more gold, it costs more per gram. That does not make it the "better" gold — you are simply buying more gold in each gram.

Prices used in this article: in late November 2026 the reference spot price was about RM486/g for 999 and RM445/g for 916, while jewellery shops were selling at roughly RM565/g (916) and RM615/g (999). Prices change every day — see today's price.

The bigger difference is what sits on top of the gold value. A new 916 chain usually carries a workmanship fee of RM15–50 per gram. A 999 bar carries a much smaller premium. When you sell, shops pay you for the gold content only — the fee is not returned.

Which One for Your Goal?

You want to wear it

Choose 916. Pure 999 jewellery bends and scratches with daily use, and most Malaysian designs are made in 916 anyway. To avoid losing the workmanship fee, look at preloved 916 — same gold, no fee.

You only want to store value

Choose 999 bars or wafers from a recognised refiner, or a gold savings account. You get the most gold per ringgit and the buyback is closest to spot.

You are saving a little each month

Small 999 wafers (1 g, 5 g) or a digital gold account let you buy in small amounts. If you prefer something you can hold and wear, add preloved 916 pieces as budget allows. See six ways to save gold in Malaysia.

You are buying a gift, mahar or hantaran

916 jewellery is the norm for weddings and family gifts in Malaysia. A 999 coin or bar also works as a keepsake that holds its value.

You will pay zakat on it

Both count. Zakat is worked out on the pure-gold content, so 10 g of 916 counts as about 9.16 g of pure gold. Full method: gold zakat in Malaysia.

Common Mistakes

  • Comparing 916 and 999 prices per gram without adjusting for purity.
  • Buying heavily patterned new 916 jewellery "as savings" — intricate designs can carry fees well above RM50/g.
  • Wearing 999 jewellery daily and being surprised when it loses shape.
  • Buying without a receipt that states weight and purity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is 999 gold better than 916 gold?
Not better, just purer. 999 is about 99.9% gold and suits bars and long-term storage. 916 is 91.6% gold and is harder, so it suits jewellery you will wear. Both are real gold and both resell easily in Malaysia.
Why is 999 gold more expensive per gram than 916?
Each gram of 999 contains about 9% more pure gold than a gram of 916. The price difference mostly reflects that extra gold content.
Which loses less money when I sell it?
Whatever you paid the smallest fee on. 999 bars and preloved 916 carry little or no workmanship fee, so their selling price is closer to what you paid. New 916 jewellery loses its workmanship fee when sold.
Can I wear 999 gold jewellery every day?
You can, but it is soft and will scratch, bend and lose detail quickly. For daily wear, 916 is the practical choice.
Is preloved 916 the same gold as new 916?
Yes. The gold content is identical; only the design and the fact that no workmanship fee is charged are different. A reputable seller will confirm purity with an XRF test and give a receipt.
