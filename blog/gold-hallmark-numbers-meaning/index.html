Gold Hallmark Numbers Explained

The small number stamped inside a ring or on a clasp tells you how much of the piece is gold. Here is every common number, its karat equivalent, and the marks that mean "not solid gold".

Updated November 2026 7 min read By Alpian Ali
How to read it: the number is gold content in parts per thousand. 750 means 750 of every 1,000 parts are gold — 75%, or 18 karat. Divide by 1,000 for the percentage; multiply the percentage by 24 for karats.

916 and 917 both mean 22-karat. 22 ÷ 24 = 0.9167, so some makers round down and some round up.

Other Stamps You May See

  • KP (karat plumb) — the gold content meets the stated karat exactly, not slightly under.
  • Maker's or retailer's mark — initials or a logo identifying who made or sold the piece.
  • 925 — sterling silver, not gold. 950 Pt — platinum.
  • India's BIS hallmark — the BIS logo, purity grade (e.g. 22K916) and a 6-digit HUID code, mandatory on gold jewelry sold in India.
  • UK hallmark — sponsor's mark, fineness number and assay office mark, applied by an independent assay office.

Is a Hallmark Proof the Gold Is Real?

Where an independent body applies the mark (as in the UK, or India's BIS system), it is strong evidence. Elsewhere, including on much of the jewelry sold in Southeast Asia, the purity stamp is applied by the maker or seller — so it is a claim to verify, not a guarantee. A receipt that states weight and purity, plus an XRF test, is what settles it. See how to check if gold is real.

Where to Find the Stamp

  • Rings — inside the band
  • Chains and necklaces — on or next to the clasp
  • Bangles and bracelets — inner surface or clasp
  • Pendants — on the bail (the loop)
  • Earrings — on the post or clasp

Stamps can wear away on old pieces. A missing stamp does not mean the gold is fake — it means you need a test.

Frequently Asked Questions

What do the numbers on gold jewelry mean?
They show gold content in parts per thousand. 916 means 91.6% gold (22K), 750 means 75% (18K), 585 means 58.5% (14K) and 375 means 37.5% (9K).
Is 916 the same as 22 karat?
Yes. 916 means 91.6% gold, which is 22 karat. Some makers stamp 917 for the same purity.
What does 18KGP mean on jewelry?
It means 18-karat gold plated. Only the thin surface layer is gold; the piece underneath is a base metal.
Does a hallmark prove gold is real?
Only when an independent assay body applies it. A maker's stamp is a claim that should be confirmed with a receipt and an XRF test.
What does 999 mean on gold?
999 means 99.9% gold, which is 24 karat. It is mostly used for bars, coins and wafers because pure gold is soft.
