This guide walks you through the complete registration process for becoming an SSGC (Sri Semantan Global Community) affiliate under the KESS physical gold savings platform. Whether you're registering online or at a branch, every step is covered below.

If you're new to SSGC and want to understand what it is first, read What is SSGC? Complete EN Guide 2026 before continuing.

What you need before registering

Have these ready before you start the registration form. Missing any of them will stall the process:

  • ✓ Malaysian IC (MyKad) — your IC number is required for identity verification. Foreign nationals need a valid passport.
  • ✓ Active Malaysian mobile number — for OTP verification and account notifications.
  • ✓ Bank account details — your bank name and account number, used for SCP (Semantan Commodity Point) withdrawals later.
  • ✓ Authorised referral link — SSGC registration is processed through the referral system. Use the link on this page if you don't have one from an existing member.
  • ✓ Payment method — online banking (FPX) for online registration, or cash/transfer at a branch.

Note on minors: If you are under 18, you cannot register independently. A parent or guardian must be the account holder. Contact your upline or the nearest SSGC branch to discuss the options available.

Step-by-step SSGC registration process

Registration is done online at emassrisemantan.com through an authorised referral link. Here's exactly what to do:

1
Click an authorised SSGC referral link

Use the Register SSGC Account button on this page — it is an authorised affiliate link that connects you to the correct registration portal at emassrisemantan.com.

Do not search for SSGC directly on Google and register through an unverified page. The referral link determines your upline (the member who supports your account), so using the right link matters.

💡 Bookmark this page before clicking, so you can refer back to this guide while registering.
2
Complete the online registration form

Fill in your personal details as they appear on your IC or passport:

  • Full name (as per IC/passport)
  • IC or passport number
  • Date of birth
  • Gender
  • Address (correspondence address)
  • Mobile number (for OTP)
  • Email address
  • Bank account details (bank name + account number)

Double-check every field, especially your IC number and bank account number — errors here will delay SCP withdrawals later.

⏱ This step takes about 10–15 minutes if you have all the information ready.
3
Verify your mobile number via OTP

An OTP (One-Time Password) will be sent to the mobile number you registered. Enter it to verify your number and proceed to the next step.

If you don't receive the OTP within 2 minutes, tap Resend OTP. Make sure the mobile number you entered is correct and active — SIM cards on DNAA (barred outgoing) status may not receive OTPs.

4
Choose your gold savings package

Select a starting package. Packages are priced in grams of 999.9 pure gold at the current market rate. The gram price changes daily with the gold market, so the RM cost of a package will vary slightly day to day.

Your upline can advise which package suits your budget and goals. As a general rule: start with what you can commit to consistently, rather than the largest package available.

📞 Not sure which package to choose? Contact your upline or the nearest SSGC branch before confirming.
5
Make payment

Payment options depend on how you're registering:

  • Online (FPX): Pay directly via online banking through the registration portal. Most major Malaysian banks are supported (Maybank, CIMB, RHB, Public Bank, etc.)
  • At a branch: Pay by cash or bank transfer at your nearest SSGC branch. Bring your payment reference from the online form if you started online.

Always keep your payment receipt. Take a screenshot of the successful payment confirmation page and save it. You may need it if there's a delay in account activation.

6
Account activation — you're in

Once payment is confirmed, your SSGC account is activated — usually within 1–2 business days. You'll receive an SMS or email notification.

Upon activation, your account will show:

  • Your gold balance (the package you purchased + 0.1g free gold promo if applicable)
  • Your SCP balance (starting at 0, grows over time)
  • Your rank: Assistant Manager
  • Your referral link (to share with others if you want to build your network)
🎉 New 2026 registrations: 0.1g of free gold is credited automatically. No action needed on your part.

What happens after you register

Registration is just the start. Here's what to do in the first week after your account is activated:

Day 1 — Log in to the member portal
Visit emassrisemantan.com and log in with your registered credentials. Verify that your gold balance and 0.1g free gold are showing correctly. If anything looks wrong, contact your upline immediately.
Day 1–3 — Contact your upline
Your upline is your primary support contact. Connect with them on WhatsApp or at a branch. They will walk you through how the platform works, what SBB (Special BuyBack) is, and what to do next.
Day 3–7 — Understand your SCP and SBB options
Learn how SCP (Semantan Commodity Points) accumulate in your account and explore whether you're eligible to activate an SBB contract. The 5 SCP withdrawal channels are scheduled to be fully operational from December 2026.
Ongoing — Track your gold balance
Log in regularly to check your gold balance, SCP balance and any promotions. If you choose to build a referral network, your member portal also shows your downline structure and their activity.

The 0.1g free gold promotion (2026)

All new SSGC affiliates who register in late 2026 receive 0.1 gram of 999.9 pure gold credited to their account upon successful activation. Here's what you need to know:

  • No action required — the 0.1g is credited automatically, not manually claimed.
  • What it's worth: At current 999.9 gold prices (approximately RM430–RM500/gram in late 2026), 0.1g is worth roughly RM43–RM50.
  • It counts as your gold balance — it appears in your account alongside the gold from your chosen package.
  • Promotion terms — the promotion applies to new registrations. It may be time-limited. Verify with your upline or at the nearest branch whether it is still active at the time you register.

Practical note: 0.1g on its own is a small amount — its real value is as a head start on your gold savings journey, not as a standalone benefit. Think of it as SSGC's welcome gift on top of whatever package you choose.

Frequently asked questions

What documents do I need to register as an SSGC affiliate?

You need: (1) Malaysian IC (MyKad) or valid passport, (2) an active Malaysian mobile number for OTP verification, (3) your bank account details (bank name + account number) for future SCP withdrawals, and (4) an authorised SSGC referral link. You do not need to upload a photo of your IC for online registration — just the IC number.

How long does the SSGC registration take?

The online form itself takes about 10–15 minutes if you have all your information ready. Account activation happens within 1–2 business days after your payment is confirmed. You'll get an SMS or email notification when your account is live.

Can I register SSGC without a referral link?

SSGC uses a referral-based registration system, so you need an authorised referral link to register. If you don't have one from an existing member, you can use the referral link on this page — it is an authorised affiliate link connected to Alpian Ali as upline.

What is the minimum amount to join SSGC?

Packages are priced in grams of 999.9 gold at the daily market rate — there is no fixed RM minimum that stays constant. The entry-level package amount is set by SSGC and varies by promotion period. Contact your upline or the nearest branch for the current package list. As of late 2026, new registrations also receive 0.1g of free gold on top of the package purchased.

What is my rank after joining SSGC?

All new SSGC affiliates start at Assistant Manager rank. This is the entry rank for everyone regardless of which package they buy. Your rank advances as your gold savings grow and your referral network expands — higher ranks unlock higher SCP earning rates and additional benefits.

What is SCP and when do I start earning it?

SCP stands for Semantan Commodity Point (1 SCP = 1 USD). You earn SCP through your gold savings milestones and community referrals. Your SCP balance starts at 0 when you first register and grows over time as you meet savings criteria and (optionally) build a referral network. From December 2026, SCP can be withdrawn through 5 channels including cash, bank transfer, USDT, Visakad SS Pay, and 50% SCV credit. Read our SCP explainer for details.

I registered but haven't received my activation. What should I do?

If it has been more than 2 business days since your payment was confirmed and your account is still not active: (1) check that your payment was actually successful — verify with your bank that the amount was debited; (2) contact your upline with your registration name, IC number and payment receipt; (3) visit the nearest SSGC branch with your IC and payment receipt. Activation delays are usually due to payment confirmation issues, not a problem with your application itself.
